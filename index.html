About This Map

This map displays sampling locations from the most recent eight days of Florida’s red tide monitoring program. The data show the abundance of Karenia brevis, the microscopic alga responsible for Florida red tide. Data are provided by the Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission (FWC). Click a sampling location to explore its collection date, abundance category, and sampling depth. Adapted from Esri’s Intro to MapView sample, this app changes the map extent, basemap, symbols, and title, and adds sampling data, components, filters, and charts.
